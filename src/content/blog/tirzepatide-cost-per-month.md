# How Much Does Tirzepatide Cost Per Month? (EU, 2026)

Cost per month is the number people actually want, and it's the number every dealer avoids because it exposes them. So here it is, with the arithmetic shown.

Two things drive the figure: the dose and the price per milligram. You control the first, the market controls the second.

## The arithmetic

A month is four weekly doses. Multiply your weekly dose by four to get the milligrams you need per month.

## The number that surprises people

A 10mg vial sounds like a month. At a 2.5mg weekly dose it is a month, exactly. At 15mg weekly it is five days.

This is the most common budgeting error in the whole market, and it's why people run out mid-titration and pay rush shipping on a single vial. Work out your monthly milligram requirement before you order anything, not after.

## Against the pen

Mounjaro and Zepbound are priced per pen pack, and pharmacy pricing varies by country, dose and whether you're paying privately. The comparison only holds per milligram, and at every dose the vial is materially cheaper per milligram.

That gap is the entire reason a research vial market exists. You are trading a marketing authorisation for a synthesis run and a COA.

## How to spend less without cutting dose

**Buy at the dose you're actually on.** Titrating up because the pen schedule says so costs money. If 5mg is working, 5mg is cheaper than 7.5mg forever.

**Buy the larger quantity only when you'll use it inside the shelf life.** Dry vials keep for months, so a multi-month order is fine if it's sealed and cold. Reconstituted vials are four to six weeks — don't mix six months of supply.

**Buy at the low end of the band on the first order.** Learn the handling on a cheap vial before you commit to a bulk order of the expensive one.

**Ask for the batch before paying.** Not a saving directly, but the cheapest way to not lose a whole order to a bad batch is to check the COA first.

**Combine shipping.** Per-vial shipping on single-vial orders is where small buyers bleed. One order a month beats four.

## What you should not do

**Chase the cheapest quote.** Wildly under-band is a different lab, not a bargain. A 10mg vial at 30 € exists; it usually isn't tirzepatide at 99% purity.

**Split vials across months by re-freezing.** You can't freeze a solution. Mix one vial at a time.

**Assume the price is fixed.** Bands move with batch and with the euro. Ask for the current number rather than budgeting from a blog post — including this one.

**Forget shipping and VAT.** Switzerland, the UK, the UAE, the US and Canada carry local VAT on arrival. Intra-EU clears undeclared, so no customs and no duty.

## A realistic first month

If you're starting, budget for the 2.5mg month: one vial, 48 € to 65 €, plus shipping. That's the honest entry cost, and it's the number to plan around. Everything above it is a decision you make later, when you know how you tolerate it.
