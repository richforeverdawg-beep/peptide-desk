Every peptide list online is a sales page. Nothing is ever described as a bad idea, which is strange, because half of the catalogue is the wrong purchase for most people.

This is the honest version: what to skip, why, and what the money is better spent on. All of these are still sold here. Some are just the wrong first buy.

## Skip: retatrutide, if it's your first order

Best trial effect of anything on the shelf — 28.3% at 80 weeks in TRIUMPH-1 — and the worst choice for a beginner.

It has no approval anywhere. No label, no licensed dose, no titration schedule. Every "protocol" you'll read is a study arm. It's also the most expensive thing here at 7.00 € to 9.50 € per milligram, so mistakes cost the most.

Get it once you know how you handle a GLP-1 and know your own titration. Not before.

**Instead:** tirzepatide. Licensed, half the price per milligram, and a label to compare yourself against.

## Skip: semaglutide, if you're cost-driven

Semaglutide is the name everyone knows, which makes it the default first buy. It's also the weakest of the three GLP-1s in trials and the most expensive per milligram here. Per vial it looks cheap only because the vial is 5mg.

**Instead:** tirzepatide, at 4.80 € to 6.50 € per milligram against semaglutide's 6.40 € to 8.80 €. If you specifically want semaglutide because you know it, buy it — but not on the assumption it's the budget option.

## Skip: Melanotan-II if you're impatient

It works. It's also the peptide with the most complaints, and almost all of them are the same complaint: nausea and a flush on the first few doses, and a tan that needs UV to develop.

People buy it expecting a tan from injection alone. You still have to sit in the sun.

**Instead:** nothing — there's no substitute for it. Just buy it knowing what it does and what it doesn't.

## Skip: anything you can't finish inside the shelf life

Not a peptide, a purchasing pattern. A bulk order at a good price is a saving only if it's used. Twenty vials you can't get through in four months is a fridge full of decaying powder, and a reconstituted vial is four to six weeks.

**Instead:** buy two months at a time until you know your real consumption rate. Then buy to it.

## Skip: the stack, first time

BPC-157 and TB-500 together is a coherent argument — angiogenesis then cell migration. It's also two sets of handling, two COAs and double the cost, on top of learning to reconstitute at all.

**Instead:** one of them. BPC-157 if the model is local, TB-500 if it's systemic. Add the second once the first is routine.

## Skip: GHK-Cu injected, if you want skin results

GHK-Cu is genuinely interesting and genuinely blue. It's also the peptide where the topical route has the better argument for the thing most buyers want it for, and injecting it means reconstituting a 50mg vial and getting the concentration right for a copper peptide that's less stable in solution than most.

**Instead:** the same peptide, applied. Or accept that injected GHK-Cu is a systemic play, not a skin one.

## Skip: Ipamorelin on its own

Ipamorelin is the cleanest GH secretagogue on the shelf and it's cheap. It's also short-acting and rarely run alone — CJC-1295 is the pairing, because one is a pulse and the other is the amplitude.

Buying Ipamorelin solo is buying half a protocol.

**Instead:** Ipamorelin and CJC-1295 together, or neither yet.

## Skip: anything with a 99.9% purity claim and no COA

Not a compound, a red flag. 99.9% is achievable and it costs, so it's usually the number a cheap vial prints without the analysis behind it.

**Instead:** whatever the batch certificate says. 98%+ with a lab name and a matching batch ID beats a 99.9% sticker.

## Skip: the cheapest quote on the page

Tirzepatide at 30 € for 10mg exists. It's a different lab, sometimes a different molecule, and always a different risk. The band on this site is 48 € to 65 € and it's published for a reason.

**Instead:** the middle of the band, from a dealer who'll send the COA first.

## What this list is really saying

Buy the boring option. One compound, a licensed one if you can, from a dealer with a certificate, at a price inside the band, in a quantity you'll finish.

Novelty is what you buy second.
